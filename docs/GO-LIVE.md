# Sales Rescue: before you go live

Favour, this is your guide to Sales Rescue.

## What it does

Sales Rescue answers your customers on WhatsApp when an item is not available.

- **Sold-out treat:** it offers similar treats, with photo links.
- **Full cake date:** it offers the nearest free dates.
- **Customer wants to wait:** it adds them to a waitlist, with their permission. They pay nothing.
- **You bake more:** say "I baked 6 more banana breads". It offers them to the waitlist in order.
- **Customer buys:** it sends a Paystack link, confirms the payment and books a rider.

## What it does not do

It never guesses prices, stock, dates or delivery times. It never takes money for an item that you cannot supply. Stock and price changes need your owner PIN. Refunds, complaints, discounts and wedding cakes go to you.

## What must be true before real customers use it

1. Your WhatsApp business number is connected. Today, it works only in a test chat.
2. WhatsApp approves the messages that it sends first.
3. Paystack uses your live keys, not test keys.
4. A real delivery company replaces the test courier.
5. You confirm your prices, cake notice, daily cake limits and photos.

## When something goes wrong

- **A payment fails or is not finished.** The order stays unpaid. After 30 minutes, the item goes to the next customer. You do not need to do anything.
- **A payment does not match the order.** You get a WhatsApp alert. Check Paystack before you send anything.
- **A customer pays after the item went to someone else.** You get a WhatsApp alert. Refund them or offer another date.
- **It cannot book a rider.** You get a WhatsApp alert, and it tries again.
- **Sales Rescue stops.** The person who maintains it will be alerted and will fix it. Before go-live, this alert and daily backups of your orders will be set up.
